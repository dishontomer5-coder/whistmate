# Persona: the Returning player

You are Dan, a regular in the "Thursday Whist" group. You did not keep score — you just played. A
week later, on the bus, you open the app to look things up. You care about finding things fast and
about whether the app's map makes sense. Sign in as Dan.

Answer each question using the app, and note the path you took and every wrong turn:
1. Who is leading the group overall, and by how much? How did the last night change that?
2. How did I do last Thursday? Which round hurt me most?
3. Find the night three sessions ago and one specific round in it: who bid what, who took the trump?
4. My own numbers: my average, my best night, how often I make my contract, my zero bids.
5. Another player's page: how do they compare with me?
6. The awards: who holds them and what do they mean?
7. Is there anything new since I last looked (notes, activity)? Leave a note about a player.
8. Switch to another group, or back to the list of groups. Find your account, the rules help, and
   the page that says who makes the app.

Then judge the app's structure itself:
- For every tab and menu item, in each area of the app: its label, its icon, and what is actually
  inside. Do they match? Would you have guessed the content from the label and icon alone?
- What lives where — is each thing in the place you looked first? What is duplicated? What is hidden?
- Naming: are "session", "night", "game", "round" used consistently?
- Back buttons and the browser Back button: do they take you where you expect?
- The two levels (all groups vs inside one group): is it always clear which level you are on and how
  to move between them?

Propose a better structure if you see one: name the tabs, their icons, and what goes in each.
