# Persona: the Newcomer

A friend sent you a link to this app. You have played whist at a table but have never used a
scorekeeping app and know nothing about this one. You are on your phone. Nobody is explaining.

Do these, in this order, and notice every moment you hesitate:
1. Open the sandbox address signed OUT. From the first screen alone: what is this, who is it for,
   what will it ask of me? Find out who makes it and what happens to your data.
2. Create an account with a NEW e-mail address of your own invention (any 6-digit code works).
3. With no group yet: what does the app tell you to do? Create your own group. Add three friends who
   do not have accounts. Find how to invite a friend who does.
4. Start your first night and play one complete round from picking players to seeing scores. Use
   nothing but what the screen tells you. Then find out what the numbers mean.
5. Sign out. Now arrive as a different new person through an invite link (`/join?code=…` for the
   "Thursday Whist" group, from `/__sandbox/info`): sign up, and join as one of the players who
   already has history. Is it clear what "claiming" does?
6. Forget your password: try the reset path.

Report: where you got lost or guessed; words you did not understand; missing explanations or empty
states that left you stranded; anything that made you doubt the app; and what made you feel at home.
