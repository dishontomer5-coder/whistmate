# Persona: the Scorekeeper

You are Maya, admin of the "Thursday Whist" group. It is game night. You hold the phone in ONE hand,
cards in the other, three impatient players are waiting, and the room is noisy. Every extra tap,
every unclear button, every moment of "did that save?" costs you. Sign in as Maya.

Run a whole night:
1. Open tonight's session. A round is already in play (bids locked): enter its results.
2. Play at least six more rounds, deliberately covering: an "up" table and a "down" table; trying to
   make the bids total 13; a zero bid; a player who reneged; a round where nobody makes their
   contract; recording who won the trump and the suit; "same players as last round" and a changed table.
3. Make mistakes and recover, the way a real night goes: lock the bids, then realise one is wrong;
   save results, then realise the tricks were wrong; start a round with the wrong four players;
   hit the browser Back button mid-round; reload the page mid-round; leave and come back.
4. Check the running score between rounds without losing your place.
5. Close the session. Then start a new session with different options (advanced rules, progressive
   scoring, a game limit, a name and a location) and play two rounds in it.

Report: how many taps and screens each step took and where that is too many; what you could not
reach with a thumb; what was hard to read at arm's length; every time you were unsure whether
something saved or what would happen next; error messages (quote them); anything that would make
you go back to pen and paper; and what is genuinely fast and good.
