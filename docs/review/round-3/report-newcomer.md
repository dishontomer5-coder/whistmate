# Newcomer review — round 3

## Who I was and what I tried
A whist player who has never used a scorekeeping app, on a phone (390x844), with nobody explaining. Signed out, I read the landing page and About. I signed up as noa.newbie3@example.com, created "Noa's Table", added Avi, Ben and Gal (no accounts) and found the invite link. I played round 1 (bids 2/3/4/5, an up table of 14, Gal missed by one), then read the scoring help and the standings.
Signed out, I opened /join?code=NHUA1B as yoav.guest3@example.com and claimed "אבי" in Thursday Whist. I tried the forgot-password path and, in a second session, joined Noa's Table as Rachel (an existing account) through the app's own invite link. I also checked desktop and small (320x568).

## Verdict
PUBLISH: yes
Top three changes:
1. Let the user set a display name for the account, or show the player name on Me instead of the e-mail prefix.
2. Show the "Added" confirmation once, as a short toast, not under every card, and drop "it".
3. Label the two start buttons ("Pick players for round 2" / "Same table — Avi bids first") and explain or remove the unread dots.
